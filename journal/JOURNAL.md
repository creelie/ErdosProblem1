Title: The exact constant of the two-parameter Type II sieve for the Erdős–Straus equation
Authors: Deep Bhattacharjee (corresponding; itsdeep@live.com, d.bhattacharjee@erl-forschung.de; ORCID 0000-0003-0466-750X) and Ushashi Bhattacharya (rai.bhattacharya.in@gmail.com; ORCID 0009-0002-2254-3914)
MSC2020: 11D68 (primary); 11N36, 11N37, 11A25
Keywords: Erdős–Straus conjecture, Egyptian fractions, sieve methods, Euler totient function

Cover letter points:
- The paper determines W(J) = Σ 1/φ(4ua) over coprime u, a ≤ J up to O(J^{-1/2} log J), answering the first open question of Dahan, arXiv:2608.24035.
- The constant 9/(2π²) is three times the one proved there; Corollary 1.2 records the consequences in Dahan's framework and states that they rest on his unrefereed arguments.
- The paper does not claim the Erdős–Straus conjecture.
